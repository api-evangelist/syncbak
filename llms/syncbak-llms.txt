# Syncbak (Zeam Media)

> Syncbak is a Marion, Iowa media-technology company founded in 2009 by Jack Perry that built the
> streaming infrastructure U.S. local television broadcasters use to carry their over-the-air signals
> online. The company rebranded to Zeam Media in May 2024; syncbak.com now redirects to zeammedia.com.
> Syncbak publishes NO public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable contract. This file is
> generated by API Evangelist from the public record — it is not published by the company.

## What the company does

- **MediaMogul / SimpleSync** — the broadcaster-facing platform: live-stream transcoding, cloud-based
  rights resolution, geo-location authentication, cloud DVR, device filtering, content management,
  stream monitoring, and clipping. Delivers station feeds to OTT destinations including Paramount+,
  Hulu, fuboTV, Amazon, Roku, Apple TV and the NFL.
- **AdSync** — proprietary dynamic ad insertion (DAI) that connects to ad-serving systems to insert
  ads into live and on-demand streams.
- **Zeam** — the consumer hyperlocal streaming app (zeam.com, iOS/Android/CTV), launched February 2024.
- **Zeam 360 / Zeam Studios / Zeamcast** — live hyperlocal access, content production, and a
  marketplace for local broadcasts announced May 2025.

## Developer surface

There is none that is publicly reachable. As of 2026-08-29:

- No developer portal, API reference, or documentation site. The corporate site nav is About,
  Careers, News & Press, Contact.
- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, Protobuf or WSDL at any probed location.
- No MCP server and no A2A agent card.
- No GitHub organization (`syncbak`, `zeammedia`, `zeam-media` all 404) and no first-party SDK on
  npm, PyPI, RubyGems, Maven Central, NuGet, crates.io or pkg.go.dev.
- `api.syncbak.com`, `developer.syncbak.com`, `api.zeammedia.com`, `developer.zeammedia.com`,
  `docs.zeammedia.com` and `api.zeam.com` do not resolve in DNS.
- `docs.syncbak.com` resolves to Google Workspace and redirects to an internal sign-in.
- `zeam.com/robots.txt` disallows `/api/` — an internal application path, not a published API.

The platform is delivered to broadcasters and station groups under commercial agreement. Integration
is a sales conversation, not a self-serve developer motion.
